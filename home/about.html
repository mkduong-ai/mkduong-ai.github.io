About Me

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from HHU Düsseldorf, where I focused on optimization methods for responsible AI. Now, I give lectures for my own courses in the AI & Data Science master's program @ HHU. My AI journey started in 2018 when I implemented an image classification model using CNNs for cancer tissue detection. My goal is to help businesses find the right AI solutions for their needs.

My expertise lies in taking novel ML models from research to production, ensuring they deliver real business value.
