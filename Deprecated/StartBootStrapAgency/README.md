# Hello Fair World!

## About Me
I am currently a PhD student in Computer Science and find myself entangled in the enigmatic pursuit of achieving fairness in datasets. Yes, because fairness is such an elusive concept, just like finding true love or a reliable Wi-Fi signal.
When it comes to my research, I'm all about heuristics that can approximate solutions faster than you can say "PhD." I mean, why wait around for ages when we can find approximate solutions much faster that work well in practice?

You can find out more about me at my GitHub page [mkduong-ai.github.io](https://mkduong-ai.github.io).

## Projects
I currently work in the project [RAPP](https://rapp.hhu.de/en).
The primary objective of the project is to develop an academic performance prediction (APP) system capable of generating performance predictions, particularly concerning the academic success of participants while delivering equitable and fair predictions.

In this project, I take charge of coordinating the software direction and ensuring its successful development. Additionally, I have hired a dedicated research assistant who has not only created the project website but also played a crucial role in developing the [internal software](https://github.com/hhu-rapp/rapp-tool) used to automate machine learning pipelines for academic performance predictions.
