I am currently pursuing a PhD in Databases and Information Systems at the Heinrich Heine University in Düsseldorf, Germany.
I work in a research project called RAPP which stands for Responsible Academic Performance Prediction.
Within the RAPP project, my role encompasses not only development tasks but also coordination, management responsibilities, recruiting assistants, and engaging with stakeholders and data governors.

### Research

When it comes to my research, I am specialized in Fairness, Interpretability as well as Data Protection in Machine Learning. My work involves designing frameworks that enable bias mitigation from datasets while preserving the integrity of the initial data distribution. As a base, I often use heuristics that only require function evaluations to optimize an objective. This makes them more flexible and agnostic to fairness notions and normative ethics.
Furthermore, I explore the stability and statistical significance of fairness notions.
