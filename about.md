I am a PhD student in Machine Learning at the Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, Germany.
Currently, I work as a teaching assistant for the courses "*Knowledge Discovery in Databases*" (Master) and "*Databases: An
Introduction*" (Bachelor).
In the past, I worked on an interdisciplinary research project called [RAPP](https://rapp.hhu.de/en/) which stands for *Responsible Academic Performance Prediction*.
Within the RAPP project, I co-developed an [MLOps application](https://github.com/hhu-rapp/rapp-tool) and a [WebApp](https://github.com/hhu-rapp/rapp-webapp)
for stakeholders in a team.
In addition to development tasks, my role within the project encompassed coordination, management responsibilities, recruiting assistants,
writing user stories,
and engaging with stakeholders and data governors.
I supervised over 15 Bachelor's and Master's theses on a variety of topics, including
algorithmic fairness, computer vision, genetic algorithms, and optimization
applied to educational and medical/healthcare datasets.
Some theses were supervised in cooperation with companies and external stakeholders
to develop an internal product.

### Research

When it comes to my research, I am specialized in **Responsible Machine Learning** which includes *Fairness, Interpretability*, and *Data Protection*.
I develop frameworks and algorithms that mitigate bias in datasets while preserving integrity, fidelity, and privacy.
For more technical details, feel free to read my papers listed on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Ube-29YAAAAJ&hl=en).
Also check out my repositories on mitigating bias ([FairDo](https://github.com/mkduong-ai/fairdo)) and hyperparameter optimization ([grAdapt](https://github.com/mkduong-ai/grAdapt)).
